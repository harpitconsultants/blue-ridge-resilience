About

Practical resilience over security theater.

Blue Ridge Resilience is a cybersecurity research and technology initiative built around practical defensive security.

Our Focus

Research that can be used.

Blue Ridge Resilience focuses on threat intelligence, detection engineering, threat hunting, incident response, security architecture, and hands-on defensive research.

The goal is straightforward: document useful work, explain security concepts clearly, and turn research into practical improvements defenders can apply.

Blue Ridge Resilience is operated by HARP IT Consultants, LLC. The site is content-first today, with room to grow into advisory, consulting, and managed security services over time.

Welcome to Blue Ridge Resilience

Why I created Blue Ridge Resilience, what I hope it becomes, and some of the ideas behind it.

August 29, 2026•4 Min Read

Hiya, my name is Crystal!

The purpose of this site is multifaceted. The main objective of this blog and site is to help grow the cybersecurity community, give people a safe space to learn and share ideas, and continue to grow. That growth may not always seem cybersecurity related, but I think it all ties together. Over the last several years, I have read many books about leadership and personal growth. One of the things that has stuck with me all of these years is that I never want to be the smartest person in the room. Truth be told, I am never the smartest person in the room. It does not matter if the room is filled with cybersecurity experts, trades workers, or people from completely different backgrounds. Everyone has something they can teach you. Another piece of advice was recently given to me by an instructor. He said that when he walks into a classroom, he expects the students to learn from him, but he also expects to learn from them. There is always something to learn from someone or an experience. Speaking of experiences, I recently stumbled across a public speaker named Sam Demma. While I was doom scrolling, he popped up talking about advice he had received, which was to always assume positive intent.

What does he mean by that?

Well, it is pretty simple. Instead of ruining your mood by immediately thinking about someone’s actions in a negative way, shift your thinking and assume their actions had positive intent. One of the examples Sam uses is basically, “I hope the person that cut me off makes it to the bathroom, because maybe they really have to poo.” It is funny, but it also makes sense. That simple shift in thinking reminded me of some of the Stoic ideas I have read from Ryan Holiday. You cannot control other people or the events happening around you. You can control how you react to them. I think that applies to a lot more than getting cut off in traffic. It applies to leadership, work, cybersecurity, and really just life in general.

So what is Blue Ridge Resilience?

This first post is a little farther away from the cyber fun stuff. I mostly wanted to give a little behind the scenes insight into why I created this site and what I want it to become. I promise we will be doing a lot of fun cyber and techy things. There will be projects. There will be labs. We will talk about threat hunting, detection engineering, threat intelligence, incident response, cybersecurity news, and whatever else I happen to find interesting. I am sure there will also be things that do not work. That is part of learning too. I do not want this to be a place where I pretend I know everything, because I don’t. I want it to be somewhere we can share ideas, learn from each other, try things, and hopefully get a little better along the way. I hope you stay along for the ride. Take what you want from what I share, improve or change what is needed for your own application, share your own ideas, and just have fun. There is always something else to learn.

Crystal Litaker

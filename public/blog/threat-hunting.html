Threat Hunting

From hypothesis to hunt

A repeatable threat-hunting process starts with a defensible hypothesis, the right telemetry, and a clear decision about what evidence would confirm or reject it.

Build the hypothesis

Use intelligence, environmental knowledge, prior incidents, and detection gaps to define what you are looking for and why.

Turn findings into improvements

Successful hunts should produce more than a report: detections, telemetry improvements, investigation guidance, or refined assumptions.

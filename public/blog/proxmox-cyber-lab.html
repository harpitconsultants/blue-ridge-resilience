Building a Cybersecurity Research Lab with Proxmox

A practical blueprint for isolating security research, collecting telemetry, and creating a repeatable environment for defensive testing.

Blue Ridge Research•8 min read

Goals

The lab should create safe separation between normal household systems and research workloads while still making it easy to collect telemetry and reproduce experiments.

Design principles

Keep research workloads segmented, minimize unnecessary inbound exposure, snapshot aggressively, document changes, and treat telemetry as a first-class requirement.

Blue Ridge Defender Takeaways

Make the lab repeatable and observable.

  • Separate research workloads from normal household and business systems.
  • Design telemetry collection before beginning attack simulation.
  • Use snapshots to return systems to known states.
  • Document network changes, test cases, and findings.
